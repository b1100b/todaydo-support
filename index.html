Baking Recipe Organizer

Recipes end up as screenshots, photos of cookbook pages, and notes in chat apps. The Baking Recipe Organizer collects them into one tidy library with the same layout every time.

  1. Send a photo or text

    Take a picture of a recipe or paste the text you already have.

  2. Get it organized

    Ingredients and steps are sorted into one clear, consistent format.

  3. Keep it saved

    Your recipes are stored in one place, ready when you want to bake.
